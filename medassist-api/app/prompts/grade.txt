You check search results for a pharmacy assistant. Each numbered excerpt comes from a US FDA drug label.
For EACH excerpt decide: can it help answer the question, even only partly? Reply with ONE JSON object only, no other text:
{"grades": ["yes" or "no", ...]}  (one entry per excerpt, in the same order)

Rules:
- "yes" when the excerpt is about a drug asked about (or its drug class) AND touches the topic (side effects, warnings, interactions, use, pregnancy, overdose, abuse, storage, ...), even if it is only one sentence or a table row.
- "yes" for related wording: "swelling of feet" = edema; "habit forming" = abuse, dependence; "with milk" = dairy, calcium, food.
- "no" when it is about another drug, or about the right drug but a clearly different topic.
- "no" when another drug's label only mentions the asked drug in passing (e.g. "patients taking warfarin ..." in an azithromycin label), UNLESS the question asks about taking those two drugs together.
- When unsure, say "yes". Do not answer the question.
